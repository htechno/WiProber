Survey Wi-Fi coverage using your own floor plans and export projects in ESX format.

• Create projects with multiple floors and calibrate each floor plan.
• Record measurements at selected points with Stop-and-Go.
• Collect measurements along a route with Continuous mode.
• Add positioned text notes and photos.
• Import ESX projects and append local surveys while preserving untouched source archive entries.
• Export all floors together for further analysis in Ekahau.

Requires Android 11 or newer. Android requires location permission and enabled location services to retrieve Wi-Fi scan results. You mark your position on the plan; WiProber does not request GPS coordinates. For frequent measurements, disable Wi-Fi scan throttling in Android Developer Options.

Projects and measurements are stored locally. There are no ads, in-app purchases, accounts or analytics. Android system backups and storage providers chosen by the user may copy files off the device.

The application interface is in English. This version has no separate Wi-Fi scanner screen. Imported Wi-Fi and spectrum measurements remain opaque and read-only; WiProber does not provide all editing features of Ekahau.
